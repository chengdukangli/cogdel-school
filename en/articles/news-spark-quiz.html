12 Questions, One Minute: Discover the Shape of Your Spark

September 15, 2026 | Cogdel School Chengdu

You are not just one "type". But somewhere in you, there is a light that shines the brightest.

We turned Cogdel's eight-dimension growth model into a light interactive quiz: 12 random questions drawn from everyday school and life situations, about one minute to finish, no sign-up and no right answers. You immediately get your own eight-dimension radar chart and a spark-type card you can save and share.

✨ Start the Spark Quiz

Why We Built It

Our teachers record something every day — a child bravely stepping up in math class to explain their solution, a child who once struggled with vocabulary scoring 16/25 on dictation, a child studying quietly in the library during the lunch break. These moments are what we call "sparks".

We record them not to grade, but so every child knows: your light is seen. This quiz hands you the same lens. Twelve questions to look at yourself first — then another look at your child.

How It Works

1. Tap to start

No sign-up, no login — it opens right in your phone's browser.

2. 12 random questions

Everyday scenes. Go with your first reaction.

3. About one minute

No right answers, no standard answers.

4. A chart and a card

Your radar chart plus a spark-type card — long-press to save, then share.

Not one question asks about grades. They ask how you get things done, how you work with people, and how you meet change — which is exactly what the eight dimensions look at: Well-being, Learning, Innovation, Agency, Leadership, Collaboration, Vision and Stewardship.

You Will Be One of Nine Spark Types

Everyone is a different mix of the eight dimensions. We distilled these recipes into nine archetypal spark types:

The Self-Starter
You move forward even when nobody's watching
The Lifelong Learner
You never stop asking "why"
The Rule-Breaker
You turn "fine" into "better"
The Glue
Things just flow smoother around you
The Decider
You call the shots when it counts
The Anchor
You're steady — and it steadies everyone around you
The Stargazer
Others see the present; you see farther
The Backbone
So reliable, people trust you with their backs
The All-Rounder
A bit of everything — the "fill any gap" person

The result is more than a name. You will see your eight-dimension radar chart, your type's superpower and its soft spot, plus one small note we want to whisper to you. No type ranks above another — the rarity labels are just for fun. After all, meeting a Stargazer was never meant to be easy.
