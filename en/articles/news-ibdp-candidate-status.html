Cogdel School Chengdu Becomes an IB Diploma Programme Candidate School

November 1, 2026 | Cogdel School Chengdu

Cogdel School Chengdu has entered the candidacy phase for the IB Diploma Programme (IBDP) — an important step on our journey toward becoming an IB World School.

Important Notice — Candidate Status

Cogdel School Chengdu is a Candidate School* for the Diploma Programme. This school is pursuing authorization as an IB World School. These are schools that share a common philosophy — a commitment to high quality, challenging, international education that Cogdel School Chengdu believes is important for our students.

*Only schools authorized by the IB Organization can offer any of its four academic programmes: the Primary Years Programme (PYP), the Middle Years Programme (MYP), the Diploma Programme, or the Career-related Programme (CP). Candidate status gives no guarantee that authorization will be granted. For further information about the IB and its programmes, visit www.ibo.org.

What “Candidate Status” Means for Your Child

We are currently in the candidacy phase for the IB Diploma Programme. This is a developmental period during which our teachers are undertaking official IB training, our curriculum is being carefully aligned with IB standards, and we are preparing for the authorization visit.

Please note: candidate status does not guarantee that authorization will be granted, and final decisions rest with the IB Organization. We will keep all families updated on our progress in a clear and timely manner.

The IBDP Philosophy — Why We Chose This Path

The IB Diploma Programme is recognised worldwide for its rigorous academic standards and its focus on developing inquiring, knowledgeable and caring young people. During our candidacy we are embedding the IB’s philosophy — including the Learner Profile attributes — into our teaching approach.

We believe this framework will benefit our students by encouraging critical thinking, intercultural understanding and international-mindedness. These are values that align closely with Cogdel School Chengdu’s own educational vision.

Any use of the official IB Learner Profile or Programme Model graphics will include the required copyright line: © International Baccalaureate Organization.

Our Preparations — What Is Happening Now

Concrete work is already under way. Below is a snapshot of the key activities taking place during the candidacy phase.

Teacher Training

Our faculty are currently participating in official IB professional development workshops to deepen their understanding of the Diploma Programme’s philosophy, assessment and teaching approaches.

Curriculum Planning

Subject guides are being reviewed in detail. We are mapping our existing curriculum against IBDP requirements and preparing unit plans that reflect inquiry-based learning.

IB Consultant Support

We are working with an assigned IB consultant who is guiding the school through every stage of the candidacy process and preparing us for the authorization visit.

Facilities & Resources

We are expanding library and digital resources, refining laboratory and studio provision, and ensuring our timetable structure can support the full Diploma Programme once authorized.

What This Means for Admissions

Students enrolling during our candidacy phase will follow a preparatory programme that is carefully aligned with IBDP standards and approaches to teaching and learning. However, official IBDP enrolment and examination registration will only be possible if and when our school receives full authorization from the IB Organization.

We are committed to transparent communication with all families throughout this process so that everyone can make informed decisions.
